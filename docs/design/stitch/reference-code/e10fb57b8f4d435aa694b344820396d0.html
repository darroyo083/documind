What are the termination conditions outlined in the Acme Corp Master Service Agreement?

Analysis Complete

Based on the analysis of the Acme Corp MSA, termination conditions are governed by Section 4 (Term and Termination). Either party may terminate the agreement under the following primary conditions:

  • Convenience: With a 60-day prior written notice [1].
  • Material Breach: Immediately, if a breach is not cured within 30 days of receiving written notification [2].
  • Insolvency: Immediately upon filing for bankruptcy or assignment for the benefit of creditors [3].

Additionally, specifically for the Provider, termination is permitted if payment is delayed beyond 45 days past the due date [4].
